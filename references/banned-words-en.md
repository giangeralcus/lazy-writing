## Generic CTAs (antislop R-15)

"Get Started" / "Learn More" / "Try Now" / "Explore" / "Discover". Name the action and its result instead: "Email Operations", "Check the Vessel Schedule".

## Structural tells

1. **Participial tack-on** (the most common tell): "The team launched the product, revolutionizing the industry." A comma plus an -ing tail that adds no information: delete the tail or make it a second sentence.
2. **Rule of three**: three adjectives, three bullets, three clauses by default. Use two, four, or one.
3. **Negative parallelism**: "Not just X, but Y". State what it is and skip the imagined correction.
4. **False range**: "From intimate gatherings to global movements", where no real scale exists.
5. **Uniform sentence length**: three consecutive sentences of the same length are a measurable detection signal.
6. **Parataxis chains and staccato drama**: "Short sentence. Then another. Then another." Use connective tissue.
7. **Hedging seesaw**: pick a side; the counterpoint gets one sentence at most.
8. **Puffery and significance inflation** (Wikipedia): "marked a pivotal moment", "reflects broader trends", "setting the stage for", "indelible mark".
9. **Actorless passive**: "The decision was made to …". Name who decided.
10. **Inanimate subject with a human verb**: "The data tells us", "the dashboard understands". Say what it shows.
11. **Emoji formatting and hashtag stacks** (Buffer).
